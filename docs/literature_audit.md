## Audit conclusion

The literature establishes homogeneous QNMs in an earlier QCD-calibrated EMD realization, diffusion slowing in a different holographic critical-point EMD model, and finite-momentum spinodal dynamics in Einstein--scalar models. The audit found no source that, by itself, already combines the exact Bayesian PHA sample with validated full homogeneous and all-helicity finite-momentum spectra across stable, metastable, and spinodal branches. This is an audit finding, not yet a publishable priority claim; citation chasing and author/title searches must be repeated immediately before submission.
