# Finite-momentum perturbations

Momentum points along `z`, leaving `SO(2)` helicities. Helicity two reduces exactly to the tensor equation with the additional `-exp(-2A) k^2/h` term. Helicity one has primitive content `{H_vx,H_zx,a_x}`; helicity zero has `{H_vv,H_vz,H_zz,H_xx+H_yy,a_v,a_z,varphi}`. Their generated kernels are not present yet. This missing derivation is a hard blocker for finite-momentum spectra, critical diffusion, and spinodal claims.
