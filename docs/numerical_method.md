# Numerical method

Backgrounds are initialized from a regular horizon series and integrated in horizon-data coordinates `(phi0, Phi1)`. The reference-compatible formulation reproduces the 2023 second-order series, evolves the Maxwell field, relaxes the UV coefficient with `C' = 8[phi exp(nu A) - C]`, and stops only after the scalar and Ricci diagnostics pass. The reduced high-accuracy formulation replaces `Phi'` by the conserved charge `Q=exp(2A) f Phi'`. UV observables are not accepted from arbitrary unconstrained polynomial fits.

The reference critical-point route follows neighboring constant-`phi0` lines and minimizes their crossings. An independent route solves `det d(log T, log mu)/d(phi0, charge_fraction)=0` together with the vanishing derivative of that determinant along the Jacobian null direction. Fixed-chemical-potential lines are continued through both folds on the horizon-data surface. Their pressure ordering is obtained from `dp = s dT`, with coexistence at equal pressure. The production `141 x 121` surface and grids coarsened by factors two and four bound the spinodal-temperature shift by `0.018 MeV` and the coexistence-temperature shift by `0.0085 MeV`.

QNMs use ingoing Eddington--Finkelstein coordinates. After multiplying by the blackening factor, decoupled equations are linear pencils `M0 + omega M1`; source and regular horizon tau rows are imposed directly. The current reported Python calculation uses a single Chebyshev--Lobatto domain with a finite ultraviolet cutoff. Multidomain/source-factored discretization around the `d2 phi ~ 1` boundary layer remains an acceptance gate rather than an implemented result. The production design retains dense QZ and SLEPc shift-invert routes, but neither substitutes for an independent shooting/source-matrix check.
