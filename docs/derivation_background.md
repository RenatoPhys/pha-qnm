# Background derivation

Substitution of the domain-wall ansatz in the EMD Euler--Lagrange equations gives the four evolution equations and one Einstein constraint recorded in the manuscript. Multiplying the scalar equation by `h` removes the coordinate singularity at the horizon. The compiled fourth-order recurrence expands these regular residuals as truncated series and solves the new coefficient linearly at each order. The first two coefficients are unit-tested against the closed forms in the manuscript.
